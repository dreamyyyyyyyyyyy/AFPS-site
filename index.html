Development Process

Over the course of 3 years,

AFPS began as SeaSurfer, a lightweight trash collection system designed simply to collect and remove trash with minimal processing logic. As its limitations became clear, especially its lack of structure, prioritization, and adaptability, it was gradually refactored into a more elegant project that could prevent, detect, and mitigate flood impact rather than just sweeping up trash. Over time, these improvements culminated in AFPS, a more intelligent system that extends beyond cleanup into structured processing and decision-making.
